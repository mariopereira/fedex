FedexConsolidation.php : 35

Consolidation API: creates, modifies, confirms, retrieves and deletes consolidations, and creates and deletes the shipments of a consolidation (ship/v1/consolidations).

Creating, modifying, confirming and deleting change real data in FedEx, and creating a shipment of a consolidation buys its labels. A request that fails because of a timeout or a network error may have been processed anyway: such a failure must be checked in FedEx before it is repeated, and this library never repeats those requests on its own. Only getShipmentResults(), getConfirmationResults() and retrieveConsolidation() change nothing and can be repeated (see FedexClient::setRetryPolicy()).

How a request is checked. schemas/consolidation.json contradicts its own sample payloads in the nested objects: only 3 of its 19 samples pass the whole of its schema (for example the schema requires consolidationKey.date and type, and the samples send consolidationType and shipDatestamp instead). So a request is checked at its top level only: the required fields, the names of the fields and their types, with each nested object or list accepted as it comes, and FedEx checks the rest and answers with its errors. checkRequest() and checkResponse() check the whole of the schema and report what does not match, without stopping anything
